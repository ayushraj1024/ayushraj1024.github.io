Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026
Website: https://ayushraj.dev

This Privacy Policy describes how Ayush Raj ("I", "me", "my") handles information in connection with my personal website and related personal automation tools, including integrations with Google Blogger, LinkedIn, WordPress, and GitHub. These tools are used only by me and are not offered as a public commercial service.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to visitors of this website and explains how my personal publishing tools handle data. These applications do not collect information from other LinkedIn members, Google users, or website visitors for marketing purposes.

2. Information I collect

Website visitors

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and your browser may collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and access logs as part of normal web hosting. I do not operate additional analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

Google / Blogger OAuth

When I use my Blogger publishing tools, the application may access:

  • My Google account information needed to authenticate with Google OAuth
  • Blogger blog content and metadata through the Blogger API
  • Public WordPress post data from sites I choose to export

Google OAuth tokens are stored locally on my own computer. I do not operate a server that collects Google credentials or blog content from other users.

LinkedIn OAuth and API

When I use my LinkedIn publishing tools (including any LinkedIn Developer application registered by me), the application may access and use the following LinkedIn-related information for my authenticated account only:

  • Basic profile information available through LinkedIn OAuth (such as name, profile identifier, and profile URL)
  • Email address, if I grant the email scope during authorization
  • Permission to create posts on my personal LinkedIn profile through the w_member_social scope
  • Post content that I choose to publish (text, links, and optional media)

I do not use LinkedIn integrations to access LinkedIn messages, connection lists for unrelated purposes, or data from other LinkedIn members beyond what is required to authenticate and publish content on my own profile.

LinkedIn OAuth tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) are stored locally on my devices. I do not sell, rent, or share LinkedIn member data with third parties.

3. How information is used

I use information only to:

  • Display and maintain this website
  • Export content from WordPress or local Markdown files
  • Create, update, or publish posts on my Blogger blog
  • Create and publish posts on my personal LinkedIn profile
  • Schedule or queue content for my own publishing workflows
  • Maintain authentication for my personal CLI and automation tools

I do not use LinkedIn or Google data for advertising, profiling, lead generation, or any purpose unrelated to my own content publishing.

4. Third-party services

This website and my tools may interact with:

  • LinkedIn API — for authenticating my account and publishing posts to my profile
  • Google / Blogger API — for blog publishing and authentication
  • WordPress.com / WordPress REST API — for reading published posts
  • GitHub — for hosting this website and optional gist-based workflows

Use of third-party services is also subject to their respective policies, including the LinkedIn Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention

OAuth tokens, exported content files, and scheduled post queues are kept locally for as long as needed to complete publishing tasks. I may delete them at any time. Website hosting logs are retained according to GitHub's policies.

6. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect OAuth credentials, token files, and exported content on my local devices. Because these are personal tools, I do not guarantee enterprise-grade security. OAuth tokens and API credentials should be kept private and must not be shared.

7. Your choices and access revocation

If you are a visitor to this website, you can control cookies and site data through your browser settings.

If you previously authorized one of my applications (for example during testing), you can revoke access at any time:

  • LinkedIn: Permitted services in your LinkedIn account settings
  • Google: Google Account permissions

8. Children's privacy

This site and related tools are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
