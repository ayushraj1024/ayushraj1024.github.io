Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026
Website: https://ayushraj.dev

This Privacy Policy describes how Ayush Raj ("I", "me", "my") handles information in connection with my personal website and related tools, including the WP to Blogger CLI (a command-line utility used to export WordPress posts and publish them to Blogger via the Google Blogger API).

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to visitors of this website and to me as the sole user of my personal CLI tools. These tools are not offered as a public commercial service.

2. Information I collect

Website visitors

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and your browser may collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and access logs as part of normal web hosting. I do not operate additional analytics or advertising trackers on these legal pages.

CLI and Google OAuth

When I use the WP to Blogger CLI, the application may access:

  • My Google account information needed to authenticate with Google OAuth
  • Blogger blog content and metadata through the Blogger API
  • Public WordPress post data from sites I choose to export

OAuth tokens (such as access tokens and refresh tokens) are stored locally on my own computer. I do not operate a server that collects credentials or blog content from other users.

3. How information is used

I use information only to:

  • Display and maintain this website
  • Export my WordPress posts
  • Create, update, or publish posts on my Blogger blog
  • Maintain authentication for automated CLI workflows

I do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

4. Third-party services

The CLI and this site may interact with:

  • Google / Blogger API — for blog publishing and authentication
  • WordPress.com / WordPress REST API — for reading published posts
  • GitHub Pages — for hosting this website

Use of Google services is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention

OAuth tokens and exported JSON files are kept locally for as long as needed to complete migration or publishing tasks. I may delete them at any time. Website hosting logs are retained according to GitHub's policies.

6. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect OAuth credentials and exported content on my local devices. Because this is a personal tool, I do not guarantee enterprise-grade security. Do not share OAuth credentials or token files with others.

7. Your choices

If you are a visitor to this website, you can control cookies and site data through your browser settings. If you authorized a Google OAuth application associated with this project, you can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

8. Children's privacy

This site and related tools are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
